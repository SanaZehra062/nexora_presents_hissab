# Hisaab
Type your spending the way you'd text a friend. Hisaab works out where your money went.

**Team Nexora:** Sana Zehra (lead), Fatima Hassan, Aleeza Mujahid

## What it is

Most students never track their spending, because every expense app wants a form filled in after every chai and every rickshaw ride. Hisaab lets you type it the way you'd text a friend, in Roman Urdu, English or a mix. The AI splits it into entries, you check them, and they're saved. A dashboard then shows where your month's money went.
